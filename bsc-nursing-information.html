BSC NURSING ENTRANCE EXAMS - OVERVIEW

BSc Nursing is a professional undergraduate nursing programme for students who want to build a career in healthcare, hospitals, clinical care and allied medical services. Admission routes may vary by institution and state. Important pathways include AIIMS BSc Nursing entrance examination, NEET UG based admission for selected nursing programmes, and state/university-level nursing entrance tests.

General Eligibility Criteria

Common Eligibility For BSc Nursing Admission
Educational QualificationClass 12/equivalent with science subjects. Most nursing entrance pathways require Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English.
Minimum MarksMinimum qualifying marks vary by institute, category and examination authority. Students should check the latest official prospectus for the exam they are applying for.
Age CriteriaAge rules vary by exam and institution. Candidates must follow the specific eligibility criteria issued by the concerned admission authority.
Admission RouteAdmission may be through AIIMS entrance, NEET UG based counselling, state nursing entrance tests or university-level admission processes.

ABVMU B.Sc. Nursing Syllabus & Exam Pattern (CNET)

The ABVMU B.Sc. Nursing syllabus for the Common Nursing Entrance Test (CNET) evaluates foundational medical sciences and logical reasoning capabilities. The exam structure evaluates key academic branches with balanced weight distributions.

ABVMU CNET Exam Layout
Exam StructureThe exam contains 120 objective questions (24 questions per subject area).
Standards AppliedPhysics, Chemistry, and Biology sections are mapped directly to Class 11 & Class 12 standard benchmarks.
Subject-Wise Syllabus Breakdown
1. Physics
  • Mechanics, Units, and Dimensions
  • Work, Power, Energy, and Thermodynamics
  • Nature of Light, Reflection, Refraction, and the Human Eye
  • Current Electricity, Electrostatics, and Electromagnetic Induction
2. Chemistry
  • Physical Chemistry: Structure of Atom, States of Matter, Solutions, and Chemical Kinetics
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Chemical Periodicity, p-block Elements, and Coordination Compounds
  • Organic Chemistry: Basic Nomenclature, Biomolecules, Polymers, and Alcohols/Ethers
3. Biology
  • Botany: Plant Cell Structure, Mendel's Laws, Ecosystems, and Plant Physiology
  • Zoology: Human Physiology (Digestive, Respiratory, Circulatory, and Endocrine Systems)
4. Nursing Aptitude
  • Basic First Aid, Health & Hygiene
  • Logical Reasoning, Decision Making, Analytical Functions
  • Current Affairs and Basics of Indian Polity
5. English
  • Grammar (Spot Errors, Fill in the Blanks) and Vocabulary (Synonyms/Antonyms)
  • Reading Comprehension, Idioms, and Phrases
