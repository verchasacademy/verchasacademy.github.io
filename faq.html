FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about admissions, courses, scholarships and coaching programs at Verchas Academy.

What courses does Verchas Academy offer?

Verchas Academy offers NEET, IIT-JEE, CUET, B.Sc Nursing, Foundation (Class 11–12), Pre-Foundation (Class 9–10) and Board Tuition programs.

Where is Verchas Academy located?

Verchas Academy is located on Saketpuri Road, Deokali Bypass, Opposite RSS Bhawan (Saket Nilyam), Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, 224001.

Does Verchas Academy provide NEET coaching?

Yes. Verchas Academy provides comprehensive NEET preparation including classroom teaching, tests, doubt sessions and mentorship.

Does Verchas Academy provide IIT-JEE coaching?

Yes. Verchas Academy offers IIT-JEE Main and Advanced preparation programs.

Does the academy provide backup support for missed classes?

Yes. Verchas Academy operates on a Hybrid Learning Model. All offline classroom lectures are recorded, giving students access to recorded classes anytime for revision or catching up on missed sessions.

Do you have fresh batches starting soon, and what are the class timings so they don't clash with school?

Yes, we run dedicated foundation classes for NEET and IIT-JEE from 9th to 12th standard, along with target batches. New batches are announced periodically to avoid school conflicts. Please visit our website or Deokali campus in Ayodhya for current schedules and timings.

Do the teachers start teaching from the very basics, or is it only advanced problem-solving?

We start every topic from the absolute basics. We specialize in helping students from rural backgrounds, Hindi medium, and the UP Board build strong foundational concepts first before moving on to advanced, competitive problem-solving.

What kind of study materials do you provide to students to help them prepare for their exams?

We provide custom-designed Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology modules tailored by our expert faculty. Our curriculum is highly effective—for the last 4 years, 100% of the Biology questions in the official NEET exam have come directly from our Verchas Academy modules.

How do you track a student's progress? Do you conduct regular test series to check their performance?

We evaluate progress using a rigorous test series featuring monthly minor tests and quarterly major tests. To ensure a completely unbiased, high-standard national evaluation, our test series are curated by external testing agencies rather than our internal faculty.

Who are the teachers at the academy? Do you have experienced faculties who specialize in NEET and IIT-JEE preparation?

The majority of our premium faculty is brought in from major educational hubs like Kota, Delhi, Lucknow, and Varanasi. Our elite team includes PhD holders in Physics, Chemistry, and Botany, an IIT alumnus heading Mathematics, an MD (Ayu) from BHU leading Biology, and B.Tech engineers.

Do you conduct Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) at the academy? How do you keep parents informed about a child's marks and attendance?

Yes, we host formal PTMs quarterly after every major test. Additionally, detailed scorecards from all minor and major tests are instantly sent to parents via WhatsApp and text messages. We also maintain an open-door policy for parents to meet our faculty or directors anytime.

What security and safety measures are in place at the campus, particularly regarding student supervision and emergency preparedness?

Our campus features 24/7 CCTV surveillance and a biometric attendance system that sends automatic entry/exit SMS alerts to parents. For maximum safety and privacy, girls' restrooms are inside the main building, while boys' facilities are outdoors. The building includes full fire safety equipment, trained staff, and a dedicated rear fire exit.

We live outside of Ayodhya. Do you provide hostel or lodging facilities for out-of-town students who want to study at your academy?

Yes, we cater to many out-of-town students. Verchas Academy has official tie-ups with premium, highly secure boys' and girls' hostels located within walking distance of our campus. These hostels feature well-ventilated rooms and an integrated, healthy tiffin meal service.

What is the fee structure for the NEET and IIT-JEE coaching programs at your academy?

We offer premier education at a highly competitive rate. Given the elite credentials of our faculty from hubs like Kota and Delhi, our one-year fee structure is among the lowest and most affordable in the region. Please visit our campus office for exact program pricing.

Do you offer any scholarships or fee concessions for meritorious students? Is there a scholarship test we can take?

Yes! We conduct a mandatory scholarship test for every student during admission. Students who score 75% or higher on this test can earn massive fee concessions of up to 70% off their overall coaching fees.

Do you offer any special fee concessions or discounts for the children of security personnel or underprivileged families?

Yes, we offer exclusive fee concessions for the children of defense, police, and security forces. Furthermore, we are committed to ensuring financial constraints do not block a bright future, offering special need-based concessions for students from economically weaker backgrounds.

What happens if I get stuck on a difficult problem or don't understand a concept during lectures? Do you have a dedicated doubt center?

We run a dedicated, full-time Doubt Solving Center right on campus. It operates continuously from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM every day. Expert teachers are physically present throughout these hours to give students one-on-one help with conceptual queries or complex numerical problems.
